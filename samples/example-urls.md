## How to Use

1. Copy any of the URLs above
2. Paste them into the "Enter video/image URL" field in the Playlist panel
3. Select the media type or leave it as "Auto-detect"
4. Click "Add" to add it to your playlist
5. Click "Play" to start playback

## Tips

- Videos will automatically play through their full duration
- Image duration can be adjusted in the Settings panel (default: 5 seconds)
- Use the keyboard shortcuts:
  - Space: Play/Pause
  - Right Arrow: Next item
  - Left Arrow: Previous item
- Playlist and settings are saved automatically in your browser's local storage
